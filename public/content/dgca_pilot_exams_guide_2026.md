# DGCA CPL Ground Exams: Syllabus, Portal Registration, and Strategic Preparation

Becoming a commercial pilot in India requires passing a rigorous series of theoretical examinations administered by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA). These ground exams test an aspiring aviator’s technical knowledge, decision-making ability, and comprehension of air law long before they take command of a multi-engine aircraft.

Navigating the DGCA exam system requires more than just memorizing facts—it demands a clear understanding of the eGCA portal, exam structure, calculation techniques, and subject priorities.

---

## 1. Requirements Before Appearing for DGCA Ground Exams

Before booking an exam slot on the official Pariksha portal (`pariksha.dgca.gov.in`), candidates must meet foundational eligibility and administrative requirements.

*   **Educational Qualification:** Minimum 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics from a recognized board (or equivalent certification). DigiLocker integration now streamlines Class 10/12 certificate verification for CBSE students.
*   **DGCA Class 1 or Class 2 Medical Clearance:** Candidates must possess a valid Medical Assessment issued by the DGCA.
*   **DGCA Computer Number:** A unique ID issued through the eGCA portal (`egca.gov.in`). This serves as the lifelong identifier for all DGCA licensing and examination records.

---

## 2. Subject Breakdown for CPL Ground Exams

To qualify for a Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL), student pilots must pass written examinations across core theoretical subjects. Each exam consists of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) administered as a Computer-Based Test (CBT), requiring a **70% score to pass**.

| Subject | Test Duration | Exam Focus & Core Topics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Air Navigation** | 3 Hours | *Calculations & Practical Flight Planning:* Latitude/longitude, time conversions (UTC/LMT), CRP-5 flight computer usage, wind triangles, Dead Reckoning (DR), radio navigation aids (VOR, DME, ILS), GNSS/PBN, and Point of Equal Time (PET/PNR) calculations. |
| **Aviation Meteorology** | 1.5 Hours | *Atmosphere & Weather Systems:* Pressure systems, lapse rates, cloud formation, atmospheric stability, fronts, tropical cyclones, Indian Southwest/Northeast Monsoon patterns, and decoding METAR, TAF, and SIGMET reports. |
| **Air Regulations** | 1.5 Hours | *Aviation Law & ATC Standards:* Aircraft Act 1934, Aircraft Rules, Civil Aviation Requirements (CARs), ICAO Annexes (Annex 2, 11, 14), Rules of the Air, airspace classifications, and ATC communication procedures. |
| **Technical General** | 3 Hours | *Aircraft Systems & Principles of Flight:* Aerodynamics, airframes, piston/jet engines, electrical, hydraulic, and fuel systems, pitot-static and gyroscopic flight instruments, and operational limitations. |
| **Technical Specific** | 1.5 to 3 Hours | *Type-Specific Performance:* Specific details of the training aircraft (e.g., Cessna 172 or Piper Seneca), including operating handbooks (POH), V-speeds, emergency checklists, and weight and balance charts. |
| **RTR(A)** | Written + Practical | *Radiotelephony:* ICAO standard phraseology, radio procedures, distress and urgency calls, and mock ATC communication practical evaluations. *(Note: RTR(A) examination processes operate in coordination with wireless communication protocols).* |

## 3. Step-by-Step Registration Process

```
1. eGCA Student Registration
   └── Register at egca.gov.in → Submit Class 10/12 details & Medicals → Obtain Computer Number

2. Pariksha Portal Account Setup
   └── Log into dgca.pariksha.gov.in using your Computer Number → Verify profile details

3. Exam Booking & Slot Selection
   └── Select upcoming quarterly session → Pick subjects & test center → Pay ₹2,500 per paper

4. Admit Card & Testing
   └── Download admit card online → Report to designated CBT center with government photo ID
```

## 4. Strategic Preparation Strategy

Passing DGCA exams on the first attempt requires a balanced sequence rather than attempting all subjects simultaneously:

1.  **Group 1 (Theoretical Foundation):** Start with **Air Regulations** and **Technical General**. Air Regulations establishes aviation law terminology, while Technical General builds fundamental knowledge of aircraft systems.
2.  **Group 2 (Calculation & Analysis Heavy):** Pair **Air Navigation** and **Aviation Meteorology**. Navigation requires fluent proficiency with the CRP-5 flight computer and flight planning formulas. Meteorology requires daily decoding practice with METARs and TAFs.
3.  **Group 3 (Aircraft Specific & Communication):** Complete **Technical Specific** once assigned to a specific training aircraft, alongside **RTR(A)** radio procedures.

> **Key Rule:** Paper pass results remain valid for **5 years** from the date of the result declaration. Candidates should plan their flying training timelines accordingly to ensure all flight tests and licensing submissions occur within this validity window.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### How many times a year are DGCA CPL exams held?
DGCA exams are typically held quarterly (four main sessions per year), alongside occasional special attempt sessions published on the Pariksha portal schedule.

### What is the pass mark for DGCA CPL exams?
Candidates must score a minimum of **70%** in each subject to pass. There is no negative marking for incorrect answers.

### How long are DGCA CPL theory exam passes valid?
Once a candidate clears a DGCA exam paper, the pass result remains valid for **5 years**.
