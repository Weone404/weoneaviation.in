# DGCA & CPL Document Checklist: Complete Documentation Guide for Indian Pilots

Documentation is the backbone of a pilot's career in India. Every milestone—from sitting for your first aviation exam to receiving your Commercial Pilot License (CPL)—depends on the precise verification of records by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA).

A single spelling mistake, mismatched date of birth across identity cards, or incorrectly scanned mark sheet can stall your DGCA eGCA profile approval, delay your Computer Number allotment, or defer your medical assessment.

This guide details the complete documentation lifecycle for an aspiring commercial pilot in India, outlining exact requirements, formatting standards, and verification steps.

Before registering on DGCA portals, gather and review your foundational identity and educational records.

### Educational Qualification Certificates
To qualify for CPL training and DGCA exams under Rule 47A of the Aircraft Rules 1937, candidates must fulfill strict academic criteria:

* **Class 10 Mark Sheet & Passing Certificate:** Serves as official proof of age, date of birth, and parent/guardian names.
* **Class 12 Mark Sheet & Passing Certificate:** Must show passes in **Physics** and **Mathematics** from a recognized Central or State Board (e.g., CBSE, ICSE, State Boards) or an equivalent institution like the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS).
* **Board Verification Certificate (BVC):** Certain verification steps require an official verification certificate issued directly by your school board confirming the authenticity of your 10th and 12th mark sheets.

> **Critical Rule:** The spelling of your name, father's name, and mother's name must be identical across your Class 10 mark sheet, Class 12 mark sheet, Aadhaar card, and Passport. Discrepancies require an official gazette notification or sworn legal affidavit before DGCA submission.

### Identity & Address Proofs
* **Aadhaar Card:** Mandatory primary identity proof for Indian citizens registering on the eGCA and DGCA Pariksha portals.
* **Passport:** Highly recommended for all pilot candidates and mandatory for non-Indian citizens (foreign nationals, OCI, PIO). A valid passport with at least 1–2 years of remaining validity is required if you plan to undergo flight training abroad or pursue foreign CPL conversion.

## 2. Documents for DGCA eGCA Profile & Computer Number

To appear for DGCA CPL theoretical examinations (Air Navigation, Aviation Meteorology, Air Regulations, Technical General, and Technical Specific), candidates must hold a unique **DGCA Computer Number** managed via the eGCA portal (`egca.dgca.gov.in`) and the Pariksha portal (`pariksha.dgca.gov.in`).

```
                   │
                   ▼

```

### Required Files for Online Portal Registration

1. **Scanned Academic Documents:** PDF/JPG scans of 10th and 12th mark sheets (and higher education certificates, if applicable).
2. **Identity Proof:** Clear scanned copy of Aadhaar Card or Passport.
3. **Digital Passport-Size Photograph:**
   * **Dimensions:** 35mm x 45mm.
   * **Background:** Plain white background.
   * **Pose:** Frontal view with neutral expression; ears must be clearly visible.
4. **Digital Signature:** Clear signature in black or dark blue ink on a plain white paper, scanned and cropped according to portal specifications.
5. **Name Change Document (If applicable):** Official Gazette notification or Marriage Certificate if there is a legal name change.

## 3. Documents for DGCA Medical Examinations

Medical checks are split into **Class 2** (entry-level) and **Class 1** (commercial-level). Maintaining a organized portfolio of medical records ensures smooth renewals throughout your flying career.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│       Class 2 Medical File           │     │       Class 1 Medical File           │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤     ├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • eGCA Appointment / Application ID │     │ • DGCA Class 2 Assessment Sheet      │
│ • Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar/Passport)  │ ──► │ • Previous Medical Record Book / CA35│
│ • Diagnostic Reports (Blood/Urine)   │     │ • Laboratory & Specialist Reports    │
│ • ECG & Eye Refraction Prescriptions │     │ • eGCA Class 1 Application Form      │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Class 2 Medical Documentation
* **eGCA Medical Application Reference Number.**
* **Government Photo ID:** Original and photocopy of Aadhaar Card or Passport.
* **Prescription / Refraction Certificate:** From an optometrist/ophthalmologist if you wear corrective glasses or contact lenses.
* **Diagnostic Test Reports:** Complete Blood Count (CBC), Fasting Blood Sugar/HbA1c, Urine Routine Analysis, and resting ECG report signed by a qualified pathologist/cardiologist.

### Class 1 Medical Documentation
* **DGCA Class 2 Assessment Sheet:** Official assessment issued by the DGCA Medical Cell following your Class 2 exam.
* **Class 1 Application Record / CA-35 Form:** Printed copy of the eGCA Class 1 application.
* **Comprehensive Diagnostic Panel:** 
  * Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA) report.
  * Chest X-Ray (PA view) with radiologist report.
  * Abdominal & Pelvic Ultrasonography (USG) report.
  * Ophthalmology evaluation sheet (including visual fields and color vision test results).
  * Full Biochemistry profile (Lipid panel, Liver Function Tests, Kidney Function Tests).

## 4. Documents Required for CPL License Issuance

Upon completing CPL ground examinations, passing Class 1 medicals, and completing 200 hours of mandatory flight training at an approved Flying Training Organization (FTO), candidates apply for CPL issuance through the eGCA portal.

### Master Checklist for CPL Application

| Document Category | Specific Requirements | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **eGCA Digital Logbook** | Verified e-Logbook entries matching physical pilot logbook countersigned by Chief Flying Instructor (CFI). | Proof of 200 total flying hours, cross-country hours, instrument hours, and night flying hours. |
| **Flying Training Certificates** | Course Completion Certificate, Solo Flying Certificate, and Cross-Country Flight Certificates from FTO. | Authenticates training compliance as per DGCA Schedule II requirements. |
| **Skill Test Proforma Reports** | Day, Night, and Instrument Rating (IR) skill test checks signed by DGCA-approved Flight Examiners. | Confirms practical flight competence within prescribed validity periods. |
| **DGCA Exam Results** | Passing certificates/status for Air Navigation, Meteorology, Air Regulations, and Technical subjects. | Verifies theoretical knowledge compliance. |
| **RTR(A) License** | Radio Telephony Restricted (Aeronautical) license issued by WPC (Ministry of Communications) or recognized foreign equivalent. | Authorizes use of aircraft radio communication equipment. |
| **ELP Certificate** | English Language Proficiency assessment certificate (minimum Level 4 - Operational). | Satisfies ICAO and DGCA linguistic interaction standards. |
| **Valid Class 1 Medical** | Active Class 1 Medical Assessment Sheet (CA-35 / DGCA Medical Cell approval). | Verifies physical fitness at time of license application. |
| **Accident/Incident Clearance** | Clearance certificate from FTO indicating no involvement in reportable aviation accidents/incidents during training. | Mandatory safety clearing declaration. |

## Technical Formatting & File Guidelines

When uploading files to the eGCA or Pariksha online portals, follow these standard submission criteria to prevent system rejection:

* **File Format:** PDF for multi-page documents (mark sheets, logbook sheets); JPG/PNG for photographs and signatures.
* **File Size:** Strictly within specified limits on the portal (typically under 200 KB for photos/signatures and under 1 MB to 2 MB for PDFs).
* **Scan Clarity:** Color scans of original physical documents at 200–300 DPI resolution. Avoid uploading blurred photos taken on mobile phones without proper lighting.
* **Self-Attestation:** Where required by specific guidelines, ensure signatures and dates are clearly visible on photocopies before scanning.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Is a passport mandatory for applying for a DGCA Computer Number?
For Indian citizens, an Aadhaar card is accepted for Computer Number registration. However, obtaining a passport early is strongly recommended as it is required for Class 1 medical records, foreign training modules, and airline recruitment processes.

### What should I do if my name on my 12th mark sheet does not match my Aadhaar card?
You must rectify the mismatch before submitting your DGCA application. You can either correct your details with the school board/Aadhaar authority or obtain an official Gazette Notification along with an affidavit clarifying that both names belong to the same person.

### Can I apply for a DGCA Computer Number if I passed 12th through NIOS?
Yes. The DGCA accepts Class 12 certificates from the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), provided you have passed Physics and Mathematics as core subjects.

### How are flight hours submitted to DGCA for CPL issuance?
Flight hours are logged electronically in the eGCA e-Logbook system by the pilot and verified digitally by the Chief Flying Instructor (CFI) or authorized signatory of your Flying Training Organization (FTO).
