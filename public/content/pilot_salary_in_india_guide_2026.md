# Pilot Salary in India: Complete 2026 Guide

## Quick Answer

Pilot salary in India depends on rank, airline, aircraft type, roster and whether part of the pay is fixed or linked to flying hours. Training is a cost, not a wage; the first commercial salary typically begins after a successful airline hire and type-rating conversion.

## Salary overview

| Career stage | Indicative salary range | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Student pilot / training phase | ₹0 | Training is a cost, not a salary. Cadet programmes vary and must be read carefully. |
| Junior first officer | ₹1.2L–₹3.5L / month | Entry-level pay depends on airline, aircraft type and hours flown. |
| Senior first officer | ₹3.5L–₹6.5L / month | Pay rises with seniority and schedule stability. |
| Captain | ₹6L–₹12L+ / month | A major jump occurs once command is reached. |
| Senior captain / check captain | ₹12L–₹18L+ / month | Additional allowances and aircraft premiums can lift compensation materially. |

## Career progression

1. Student Pilot / CPL Phase
2. First Officer – Entry Level

## Detailed salary breakdown

A pilot’s salary is often a mix of fixed monthly pay, flying-hour-linked pay and allowances. The total range can shift materially based on aircraft type, airline policy and whether the contract is fixed-term or permanent.

## Salary components and deductions

## CPL-to-employment pathway

1. Complete Class 12 and DGCA medical screening.
2. Pass DGCA ground papers and receive the required approvals.
3. Complete flight training and build the required hours.
4. Complete aircraft type rating and airline assessment.
5. Enter induction and line training.
6. Move to first officer line release, then to command.

## Domestic vs. overseas careers

### Indian domestic airlines

Attractive growth opportunities exist, but salary and progression remain tightly tied to fleet type, airline recruitment policies and seniority.

### Middle Eastern / Gulf carriers

Headline pay can be higher in some ranks, but it must be checked against allowances, taxes and contract conditions.

### North America / Europe

Compensation structures differ and can be materially different from Indian contracts in both pay and visa arrangements.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the starting salary of a pilot in India?

The starting pilot salary in India is highly dependent on the airline, aircraft type, contract structure and whether the pay is fixed or linked to hours flown.

### Do pilots get paid during CPL training?

No. CPL training is a cost, not a wage.

### How much does a captain earn in India?

Captain salaries in India can be materially above first officer pay and often start in the low single-digit lakhs per month for narrow-body commands.

### Why do pilot salary figures online differ so much?

They often mix gross pay, allowances, incentives and different contract structures.

### Is a pilot salary in India enough to recover training costs?

It depends on the job outcome, aircraft assignment, route structure, time to command and the contract.
